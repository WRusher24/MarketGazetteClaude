## Getting Started

When you first open MarketGazette, you'll see the Dashboard which provides an overview of the market, your watchlist, and latest news.

### Initial Setup

1. **Create your watchlist**: Start by adding stocks you're interested in tracking
2. **Choose your theme**: Select from Minimalist Light, Sleek Dark, or Bloomberg Terminal themes
3. **Explore the Gazette Feed**: Browse the latest market news and analysis

## Navigation

MarketGazette features different navigation layouts depending on your device:

### Desktop Navigation
- **Left Sidebar**: Access all main sections (Dashboard, Gazette Feed, Newsroom, Watchlist, AI Analyst, Settings)
- **Top Bar**: Search, notifications, and user menu

### Mobile Navigation
- **Bottom Navigation Bar**: Thumb-friendly access to main sections
- **Hamburger Menu**: Access all sections in a collapsible menu

## Reading News

### Gazette Feed
The Gazette Feed is your personalized market newspaper featuring:
- **Top Banner Ticker**: Real-time scrolling of major indices (S&P 500, NASDAQ, Dow, Bitcoin, Ethereum)
- **Lead Story**: Today's top breaking market story
- **News Sections**:
  - *Top Headlines*: Major market-moving news
  - *Watchlist News*: News filtered to only your watchlist stocks
  - *AI Sentiment Radar*: Visual gauge of overall market sentiment

### In-App News Reader
Clicking any news headline opens an in-app reader that displays:
- Full article text
- Publisher source and publication timestamp
- Original cover image
- Option to view the original article externally
- **AI Executive Summary** button: Get 3-bullet takeaways, impact analysis, and sentiment scoring

## Watchlist Management

### Adding Stocks
1. Navigate to the Watchlist section
2. Enter a stock ticker (e.g., AAPL, GOOGL, TSLA) in the input field
3. Press Enter or click "Add Ticker"
4. The stock will be added with real-time price data

### Managing Your Watchlist
- **Remove stocks**: Click the "X" button next to any stock in your watchlist
- **Reorder**: Stocks are displayed in the order they were added
- **Real-time updates**: Prices and percentages update automatically

### Watchlist News
The Watchlist News section shows only news articles related to stocks in your watchlist, making it easy to stay informed about your investments.

## Stock Analysis

Clicking any stock ticker symbol opens a detailed analysis page featuring:

### Price Header
- Current price with real-time updates
- Change amount and percentage (green for gains, red for losses)
- Visual indicators for price movement

### Interactive Charting Suite
- **Timeframes**: 1D, 1W, 1M, 1Y, ALL
- **Chart Types**: Candlestick vs Line chart toggle
- **Technical Analysis**: Zoom, pan, and crosshair features

### Key Financial Metrics Grid
- Market Capitalization
- P/E Ratio
- 52-Week High/Low
- Trading Volume
- Dividend Yield
- Additional ratios as available

### Dedicated Ticker News Feed
- Real-time headlines filtered exclusively for the selected stock
- In-app reading experience for all news articles
- Direct access to AI analysis for each article

## AI Features

### AI Executive Summary
Available for any news article:
1. Click the "AI Executive Summary" button in the news reader
2. Get a 3-bullet summary of key takeaways
3. See analysis of potential impact on stock price
4. View market sentiment scoring (Bullish/Bearish/Neutral)

### AI Company Research & Insights
Available on any stock detail page:
1. Click "Analyze Stock with AI" button
2. Receive a comprehensive 4-part analysis:
   - Business Model & Revenue Drivers
   - Strengths vs. Key Risks
   - Recent Quarterly Earnings Performance
   - Market Sentiment & Analyst Consensus

### AI Sentiment Radar
Found in the Gazette Feed sidebar:
- Visual gauge showing overall market sentiment
- Confidence percentage
- Brief analysis summary
- Historical context when available

## Settings & Customization

### Theme Selector
Accessible from Settings or the navigation header:
1. **Minimalist Light**: Clean, high-contrast design with paper-white background
2. **Sleek Dark**: Modern dark interface with emerald/green accents
3. **Bloomberg Terminal**: Retro-chic high-density dark theme with amber/gold headers

### Notification Settings
- Enable/disable price alerts and breaking news notifications
- Toggle notification sounds
- Set alert thresholds for your watchlist stocks

### Data & Privacy
- Automatic backup of watchlist and settings
- Auto-archive inactive sessions (configurable)
- Data export options

## Mobile Usage

MarketGazette is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices:

### Navigation
- Bottom navigation bar for quick access to main sections
- Collapsible menu for all other options
- Gesture-based navigation where supported

### Reading Experience
- Optimized text sizing for mobile screens
- Images that scale appropriately
- Easy-to-tap buttons and interactive elements

### Offline Capabilities
- Recently loaded content remains accessible
- Watchlist data persists between sessions
- Settings are saved locally

## Tips & Tricks

### Keyboard Shortcuts (Desktop)
- `/` : Focus search bar
- `g d` : Go to Dashboard
- `g g` : Go to Gazette Feed
- `g w` : Go to Watchlist
- `g a` : Go to AI Analyst
- `g s` : Go to Settings
- `?` : Show help dialog

### Maximizing Your Experience
1. **Regularly update your watchlist** to reflect your current interests
2. **Use the AI features** to quickly digest lengthy articles
3. **Check the AI Sentiment Radar** for quick market mood assessment
4. **Explore different timeframes** on stock charts to understand trends
5. **Read the lead story** each day for the most important market news

### Troubleshooting
If you encounter issues:
1. **Refresh the page** to reload data
2. **Check your internet connection** for real-time updates
3. **Clear browser cache** if experiencing display issues
4. **Contact support** through the Settings section for persistent problems
